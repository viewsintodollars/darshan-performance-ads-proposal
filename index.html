Performance Ads · Scope Boundaries & Terms

What the service covers, what sits outside it, and how the fee works.

In scope

  • Audit of every existing ad account
  • GA4, Tag Manager, Pixel, Conversions API and UTM tracking
  • Merchant Center feed for the 146 SKUs
  • Meta, Google, Amazon, Flipkart and quick commerce campaigns
  • Ad creative production, static and short video
  • Landing page briefs and weekly creative tests
  • Daily management and budget reallocation
  • Weekly and monthly reports against Client's targets

Out of scope

  • Media spend, paid directly to the platforms
  • Talent, location or studio hire for shoots
  • Website and landing page development
  • Marketplace listings and catalogue work
  • Organic social, influencers and PR
  • CRM, email and WhatsApp marketing
  • Offline, print and broadcast media
  • Guaranteed ROAS, rank or revenue

Commercial terms

  • One time setup payable at kick off
  • Monthly fee is a share of total spend across all platforms
  • Rates apply to spend within each band, like tax slabs
  • A minimum monthly fee covers low spend months
  • Management fee invoiced monthly on actual spend
  • Ad accounts, pixels and data stay in Client's name
  • Fees exclude GST
